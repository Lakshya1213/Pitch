Instead of bringing the whole solar panel to the chemistry, we bring the silver-rich fraction to the chemistry.
1 · Order flips

Physical concentration comes before chemistry, not after. Most routes leach first and sort out the mess later.

2 · Early exits

Al frame, glass and the low-silver fraction leave the silver path early, each to its own recycling route.

3 · Targeted chemistry

Leaching is aimed at silver in an already enriched feed, not at dissolving every metal.

4 · Cleaner downstream

Fewer co-dissolved metals means simpler separation and better-separated waste streams.

5 · Circular supply

Recovered Ag goes back into new products, including new solar panels.
